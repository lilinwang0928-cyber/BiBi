動詞想當名詞用，有兩條路：變成 V-ing，或加上 to。難的是前面那個動詞說了算——enjoy 只收 V-ing、plan 只收 to V，而 stop、remember、forget、try 兩種都收，但意思完全不同。這一章鋪了六條功能軌，點開風景裡的每個搭配，看它為什麼選那一種。

六條功能軌

判斷順序：先看前面那個動詞是誰。它只收 V-ing、只收 to V，還是兩種都收？兩種都收的，再問這件事做了沒有。

主詞軌　當主詞視為單數

  • 動名詞當主詞：Learning English is very important.
  • 即使有複數名詞：Reading books is fun.
  • 虛主詞：It is hard to master a new language.

不定詞用法軌　修飾 · 目的 · 程度

  • 修飾形容詞：I was so surprised to see my old friend.
  • 表示目的：Tom went out in order to buy some dinner.
  • 太而不能：He was too tired to walk to the market.

只接 V-ing 軌　六個必背

  • enjoy：I enjoy reading novels on weekends.
  • finish · practice：I usually practice playing the guitar.
  • mind · avoid · keep：I try to avoid going to crowded places.

只接 to V 軌　指向未來

  • plan · decide：My family plans to take a trip to Japan.
  • hope · promise：We hope to visit many famous temples.
  • want · need：We want to try all the delicious local food.

慣用語軌　四個必考

  • be busy：My father is busy answering phone calls.
  • spend 時間：He spends a lot of time working in his office.
  • look forward to：He looks forward to seeing his family tonight.

雙義軌　兩種都接，意思不同

  • stop：We stopped to take a rest.
  • remember：I clearly remember locking the front door.
  • try · forget · regret：I regret spending so much money on it.

兩份必背清單

成員語感
只接 V-ingGERUNDenjoy · finish · practice · mind · avoid · keep已經在做、實際在進行的事
只接 to VINFINITIVEplan · decide · hope · promise · want · need還沒發生、指向未來的意圖

雙義動詞　一個口訣走完五個

接 to V 指的是「還沒做的事」，接 V-ing 指的是「已經做過或正在做的事」。五個動詞都適用。

動詞＋ to V　還沒做＋ V-ing　已經做
stop停下來，去做另一件事停止正在做的動作
remember記得要去做記得已經做過
forget忘記要去做忘記已經做過
try試圖、努力去做試試看某種方法
regret遺憾要告知某事後悔曾經做過

三條鐵律

動名詞當主詞一律單數

Reading books is fun.　主詞是「閱讀」這件事，不是 books。

look forward to 的 to 是介系詞

look forward to seeing。同類還有 be used to、object to，後面都接 V-ing。

事物 ＋ need ＋ V-ing 表被動

My car needs washing.　＝ needs to be washed。主詞是事物時才這樣用。

最常考的三組對照

stop to V　vs　stop V-ing

We stopped to take a rest.　停下腳步，去休息。
We had to stop walking for ten minutes.　停止走路這個動作。

remember to V　vs　remember V-ing

Please remember to close all the windows.　還沒關，提醒你去關。
I clearly remember locking the front door.　已經鎖了，記得這件事。

try to V　vs　try V-ing

I will try to get up early tomorrow.　努力去做，強調過程艱辛。
I might try using three alarm clocks.　試試看這個方法有沒有效。
